Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

MysteryStudio Uploader ("the app") is a private automation tool operated by its developer for their own use.

What the app accesses

The app uses YouTube API Services, and the Instagram and Facebook Graph APIs, only on accounts owned by the developer. It uses this access to upload videos and to read and reply to comments on the developer's own videos.

Data collection and sharing

The app does not collect personal data from visitors or other users. It does not sell, share or disclose any data to third parties. Public comments on the developer's videos are processed only to generate replies, and are not used for any other purpose.

Storage

Access tokens are stored as encrypted secrets in the developer's private GitHub repository. The app keeps a small log of published video IDs and handled comment IDs to avoid duplicates.

YouTube

The app uses YouTube API Services. By using it, you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy. Access can be revoked at any time at Google security settings.
